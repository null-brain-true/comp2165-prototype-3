Put replacement PNG/JPG images and MP3/OGG/WAV audio files here. You can add subfolders.
Enter each file's path in Student Content/Visuals.csv or Audio.csv.
For example, images/new-cookie.png refers to Student Content/Media/images/new-cookie.png.
Double-click Student Content/Refresh Student Content.bat to copy the files into the game,
or Student Content/Run Student Build.bat to copy and build without opening Unity.
